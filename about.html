About this site

Last updated November 9, 2026

Where the data comes from

Everything on this site comes from public records the City of Chicago publishes on its open data portal: Traffic Crashes – Crashes and Traffic Crashes – People. The Chicago Police Department files these reports, and the city updates them daily.

  • The records are provisional. Police amend reports after the fact, and a death can be added weeks after the crash, so recent numbers often change.
  • Only streets under Chicago Police jurisdiction are included. Crashes on expressways and in the suburbs are not.
  • Citywide reporting starts in late 2017, so yearly counts begin in 2018.
  • Official yearly crash statistics for Illinois are published by IDOT and can differ from these counts.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no forms, and no ads. We do not sell personal information.

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and filters people use, so we know what to improve. It sets cookies in your browser and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. We do not combine this data with anything else. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking cookies; the site works the same either way.

To show the pages, your browser connects to a few services, and each of them sees your IP address as part of a normal web request:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.
  • The City of Chicago data portal supplies the crash records.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map images.
  • Google Analytics measures visits, as described above.

Fonts and the map library are served from this site, not from third parties.

Corrections and removal requests

Records about people who were killed come from public police data and contain no names. If you are a family member and would like a specific record hidden from this site, or if you see an error, email us and describe the date and location of the crash. We respond to every request and handle removal requests about victims first.

We can hide records on this site, but we cannot change the city’s data. Corrections to the source records go to the Chicago Police Department.
